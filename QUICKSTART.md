# CyberOpoly - Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started in 3 Steps

### 1. Open the Game
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser:
- Double-click the file, or
- Right-click → Open With → Your Browser, or
- Drag and drop into your browser window

### 2. Set Up Players
- Choose number of players (1-4)
- Enter player names
- Click "Start Game"

### 3. Play Your Turn
1. **Click "Roll Dice"**
2. **Read what happens** - you'll learn about cybersecurity concepts!
3. **Take actions**:
   - Buy properties when available
   - Pay rent when landing on opponent properties
   - Draw and read educational cards
4. **Click "End Turn"**

## First Game Walkthrough

### Turn 1
- Roll the dice (you'll see two dice values)
- Your piece moves automatically
- If you land on an **unowned property** (shows price), you can buy it
- Click the property space to learn what it is!

### Understanding the Board

**Bottom Right Corner = START (SOC)**
- Collect £200 when you pass

**Property Spaces** (colored bars at top)
- Click any space to see details and learn about it
- Buy them to collect rent from other players

**Card Spaces** (Phishing Email / Security Audit)
- Draw a card and learn about a cybersecurity scenario
- Follow the card's instructions

### Your Goal
- Buy properties
- Collect rent from opponents
- Upgrade your security (like houses in Monopoly)
- Be the last player with money!

## Controls Quick Reference

### During Your Turn
- **Roll Dice** - Start your turn (only available when it's your turn and you haven't rolled)
- **Buy Property** - Purchase the property you're standing on (appears when available)
- **Upgrade Security** - Add security levels to your properties (available when you own a complete color group)
- **Mortgage** - Convert properties to cash when you need money
- **End Turn** - Finish your turn and pass to next player

### Information Panels
- **Top Panel** - Current player and their money
- **Players Panel** - All players' status and money
- **Game Log** - Recent actions and events

### Learning Features
- **Click any space** - See detailed info and educational content
- **Read cards carefully** - Each card teaches a cybersecurity concept
- **Property descriptions** - Explain real security systems
- **Educational sections** - Learn best practices and attack methods

## Pro Tips

1. **Complete Color Groups**: Own all properties of one color to upgrade them
2. **Upgrade Early**: Properties with upgrades earn much more rent
3. **Save Cash**: Keep £200+ in reserve for rent and emergencies
4. **Read Everything**: The educational content is the best part!
5. **Click Spaces**: Click any board space to learn what it represents

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I buy a property?**
A: Roll dice, land on an unowned property (no colored owner indicator), click "Buy Property" button

**Q: How do I upgrade properties?**
A: Own all properties in a color group, then click "Upgrade Security" and choose which property to upgrade

**Q: What's "Quarantine"?**
A: It's like Jail in Monopoly - your system is isolated. Roll doubles to escape, or wait 3 turns

**Q: How do I win?**
A: Be the last player with money! Others go bankrupt when they can't pay rent

**Q: Where's the educational content?**
A: Everywhere! Click board spaces, read card descriptions, and look for "💡 Learn More" sections

## Game Rules Summary

### Movement
- Roll two dice, move that many spaces clockwise
- Pass GO (SOC) = collect £200
- Roll doubles = take another turn (but 3 doubles in a row = Quarantine!)

### Properties
- **Unowned**: Buy for listed price or skip
- **Owned by opponent**: Pay rent
- **Owned by you**: No action, but you can upgrade it on your turn

### Upgrades
- Must own ALL properties in a color group
- Click "Upgrade Security" button
- Choose property to upgrade (£50-£200 per upgrade depending on property)
- 4 levels + 1 hotel per property

### Money Management
- Start with £1,500
- Collect £200 for passing GO
- Pay rent when landing on owned properties
- Can mortgage properties for cash (but they don't earn rent when mortgaged)

### Winning Condition
- Last player standing wins
- Players go bankrupt when money goes below £0 and can't raise funds
